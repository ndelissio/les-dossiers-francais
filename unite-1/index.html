Conjugaison

Every Unit 1 verb, full present-tense tables — type all six forms. (Word knowledge for these verbs lives in the Vocabulaire tab as infinitive cards.) Quick start:

  • Fill-in only: type all six forms — accents count, and each card grades once (Check or Reveal locks it).
  • Scope with Up to my level (set your chapter on the dashboard), a single chapter, or a verb group.
  • A verb turns green after 3 correct in a row; any miss resets the streak. Hide known drops a verb from the lineup the moment it's mastered; New only shows just the verbs you haven't tried yet; Rusty resurfaces known verbs you haven't touched in 14+ days.
  • The progress table below tracks everything. Honestly know one already? Hit Je connais déjà ✓ on its card after Check or Reveal — one later miss and it's back in rotation. Each chapter section has its own reset.
  • Training — first-contact reps: the conjugation stays on the card, Check gives feedback, nothing is recorded and nothing counts as a miss.

Vocabulary trainer

Every testable word from Chapters 1–4 (incl. the preliminary chapter), plus every glossaire verb as an infinitive card. Quick start:

  • Recall flips cards EN↔FR; Spelling makes you type the French exactly — accents count, taught synonyms are accepted, and each card grades once.
  • Articles are part of the answer — le/la counts, wrong or missing article is a miss. Tap 🔊 after checking to hear the word.
  • A word turns green after 3 correct in a row; any miss resets it. Hide known drops a word the moment it's mastered; New only shows just the words you haven't tried yet; Rusty resurfaces greens untouched for 14+ days.
  • The progress table below follows your chapter and topic filters. Je connais déjà ✓ (on the card, after Check or Reveal) skips what you truly know; each chapter section has its own reset.
  • Training — first-contact reps: the answer stays on the card, Check gives feedback, nothing is recorded and nothing counts as a miss.

Grammar drills

Audited against the Unit 1 textbook and workbook: every drill uses glossaire vocabulary and the structures taught at its chapter, at textbook difficulty. Each drill deals a round of 10 from a bank of ~24 — hit New round 🎲 for a fresh draw. Word banks appear where the textbook provides one. A Vocab in context drill covers each chapter; spelling practice lives in the Vocabulaire trainer. Couverture (distinct bank items answered right) and best-round scores feed the dashboard.

Quizzes & Unit 1 exam

Two tools, modeled on how your class tests. Chapter quizzes draw 10 randomized fill-in questions from that chapter's bank — a different draw every attempt. The Unit 1 practice exam mirrors the real Test 1 study guide (Chapitres 1–4, reading side): three short passages with comprehension questions in English, a Vocabulaire section drawn from the official test list, and a Grammaire section covering exactly the tested structures. Accents are graded strictly everywhere — just like class. Untimed: your real tests give you more time than you need, so take what the questions need and no more.

Pratique orale — OPI prompts

The speaking test is a conversation, and the only way to train it is out loud. These prompt cards mirror the Oral section of your study guide (yourself, family, preferences, routine, future plans, questions). Pick a card, hit a timer, and talk until it runs out — the 45 → 30 → 15 second ladder is the same drill your textbook runs in class. No grading, no recording, no listening: just reps. Say it to a partner, a wall, or your dog.
