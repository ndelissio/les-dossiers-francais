Changelog v0.2.0 (16 Sep 2026): Chapter 18 vocabulaire, grammaire & examen blanc. Built out of order, ahead of Chapter 17. Vocabulaire: 200 cards from the Chapter 18 end-of-chapter glossaire (Health, Sports, and Medicine), across 8 topic groups sized to the ~20–25-word standing rule (including a dedicated Corps/body-parts group), plus 70 mini-glossaire words folded in from reading/listening activities across the chapter's four sections (nutrition, stress, sport, technology) and the Tour de France culture reading, as flagged exceptions (own topic tags, own comment headers). A synonym collision (néfaste vs nuisible, both glossed "harmful") was resolved with distinguishing EN cues rather than a merge, since the source text treats them as distinct entries. A handful of workbook exercise words with no locatable source translation (an "intrus" exercise likely tied to an audio passage) were left out rather than guessed. Grammaire: 4 new drills — l'hypothèse sur le futur (si + présent + futur/impératif/présent), les pronoms démonstratifs (celui/celle/ceux/celles), and les pronoms relatifs composés (ce qui/ce que/ce dont) — plus a Vocab en contexte drill, all with fresh example sentences. Examen blanc: a 24-item Chapter 18 quiz bank (10 drawn per attempt) covering all three grammar points plus vocabulaire. Pratique orale: 6 new Chapter 18 prompts. Chapters 17 and 19 and etymology mémos are not yet built — this is a partial, in-progress release.
