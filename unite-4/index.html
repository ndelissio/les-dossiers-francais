Changelog v0.8.3 (10 Oct 2026): Chapter 19 vocabulary — Mémo card corrections. A line-by-line check of the Chapter 19 Mémo cards against Wiktionary, WordReference and Larousse found wrong or doubtful etymologies, false English relatives, wrong faux-ami notes and mnemonics that followed a sound-alike instead of the real root. 71 of the 166 Chapter 19 cards were edited (101 field changes).
